Frequently Asked Questions

What is an offshore LLC?

An offshore LLC is a limited liability company formed under the laws of a jurisdiction outside the owner’s home country. It remains subject to the laws, taxes, disclosures, banking rules, and reporting obligations that apply to its owners and activities.

Is an offshore LLC legal?

Yes, when formed, disclosed, funded, operated, and reported lawfully. It cannot be used to hide ownership, evade tax, conceal funds, or avoid applicable sanctions, licensing, anti-money-laundering, or reporting rules.

Which jurisdiction should I choose?

The answer depends on the entity’s genuine business purpose, owners, management, markets, banking needs, local substance, reputation, costs, legal protections, and tax consequences. No jurisdiction is automatically best.

Will an offshore LLC eliminate U.S. taxes?

No. U.S. persons are generally taxed and may have extensive reporting obligations based on ownership, classification, income, accounts, transactions, and other facts. Independent U.S. tax advice is essential before formation.

Can an offshore LLC open a bank account?

Potentially, but approval is never guaranteed. Banks review jurisdiction, owners, source of funds, business purpose, expected activity, customers, counterparties, tax records, and compliance risk.

Does USABA provide legal or tax advice?

No. USABA provides business consulting, planning support, and professional coordination. Entity formation, contracts, legal opinions, tax classifications, filings, and advice must come from qualified independent professionals.
