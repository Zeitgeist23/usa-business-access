A stronger banking application starts with complete records

Requirements vary by bank, applicant, industry and ownership structure.

Entity records

Formation document, good-standing record and governing agreement.

Ownership and control

Beneficial owners, managers, directors and authorized signers.

Identity and address

Current identification and acceptable residential and business-address records.

Business purpose

Operating plan, expected activity, counterparties and transaction profile.

Source of funds

Organized records explaining initial deposits and expected capital flows.

Professional coordination

Independent CPA, attorney, banker or compliance support when required.
