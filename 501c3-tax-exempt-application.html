Frequently Asked Questions

Is state nonprofit incorporation the same as 501(c)(3) recognition?

No. State incorporation creates the nonprofit corporation. Federal recognition of tax-exempt status is a separate IRS process.

What is the difference between Form 1023 and Form 1023-EZ?

Form 1023 is the complete application. Form 1023-EZ is a streamlined application available only to organizations that meet the IRS eligibility requirements.

How is the application submitted?

The IRS requires Form 1023 and Form 1023-EZ applications to be submitted electronically through Pay.gov.

What records should be organized before applying?

Organizations generally need an EIN, organizing documents, bylaws, governance records, activity descriptions, financial information, and other supporting materials appropriate to the application.

Does USABA provide legal or tax advice?

USABA provides business consulting and coordination and can connect clients with independent legal and tax professionals when specialized advice is needed.
