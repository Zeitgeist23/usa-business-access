International buyers need coordinated business and immigration planning

Buying a U.S. business may support certain immigration strategies, but a purchase does not guarantee visa eligibility or approval.

  • Revenue and normalized earnings
  • Licenses, leases, contracts and employees
  • Taxes, liens, litigation and compliance
  • Assets, inventory and transition obligations

Independent advice matters

USABA can coordinate the business-search and transaction process, but immigration strategy must be evaluated by a qualified U.S. immigration attorney. Buyers should also obtain independent legal, tax, valuation and lending advice.
