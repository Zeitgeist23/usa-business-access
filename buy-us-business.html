A disciplined acquisition process

The right opportunity must fit the buyer, the financing and the verified records.

Buyer criteria

Define location, industry, price, cash flow, capital and operating role.

Business search

Coordinate opportunities from brokers, owners and established marketplaces.

Financial review

Examine tax returns, statements, payroll, leases and working-capital needs.

Valuation support

Compare price, normalized earnings, assets and market evidence.

Financing readiness

Organize buyer equity, lender records and acquisition structure.

Closing coordination

Work with independent attorneys, CPAs, lenders and closing professionals.

Due diligence must verify the business—not merely the listing

  • Revenue and normalized earnings
  • Licenses, leases, contracts and employees
  • Taxes, liens, litigation and compliance
  • Assets, inventory and transition obligations

Independent advice matters

USABA coordinates the process but does not replace legal, tax, valuation or lending professionals. Buyers should obtain independent review appropriate to the transaction.
