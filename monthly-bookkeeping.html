Bookkeeping in the United States

What bookkeeping actually does for a U.S. business

Bookkeeping is the ongoing process of turning everyday financial activity into a complete, organized accounting record. It gives an owner visibility into revenue, expenses, cash, debts, and what the business owns.

1

Record and categorize sales, deposits, purchases, fees, payroll entries, and owner transactions.

2

Reconcile accounts so the books agree with bank and credit-card statements.

3

Maintain the general ledger and identify duplicates, missing records, unusual balances, and open questions.

4

Produce financial statements used to manage the business and prepare a year-end package for the tax professional.

Bookkeeping creates the financial record. Tax preparation applies tax law to that record. An audit or review provides a separate level of professional assurance.

For U.S. residents doing business overseas

Foreign operations need books that can support both local records and U.S. reporting

U.S. citizens and resident aliens generally report worldwide income. Ownership of, or activity through, a foreign corporation, partnership, disregarded entity, branch, or financial account may also create specialized U.S. information-reporting obligations. Good bookkeeping preserves the source data your international tax professional needs to evaluate those rules.

Frequently asked questions

Before you get started

Is USA Business Access the bookkeeper?

USABA manages the client relationship and coordinates the engagement. Bookkeeping work is performed by an independent bookkeeping professional assigned to your scope.

Do you work in QuickBooks Online?

Yes. The service is designed around QuickBooks Online. We confirm relevant experience and verify current ProAdvisor certification before representing an assigned professional as certified.

Why is pricing listed as “starting at”?

Bookkeeping effort changes with transaction volume, number of accounts, entity count, payroll, inventory, currencies, sales tax, AP/AR, and the condition of existing books. We confirm a fixed recurring fee after review.

Can you clean up prior months?

Yes. Catch-up and cleanup work is separately scoped after reviewing the file, the number of months involved, and the quality of supporting records.

Are tax returns included?

No. Tax preparation and tax advice require a separate engagement with an appropriately qualified independent professional.

Do I share my QuickBooks password?

No. Access should be granted with QuickBooks Online accountant permissions. USABA and assigned professionals do not need your personal login credentials.
