Frequently Asked Questions

Is forming a nonprofit corporation the same as receiving 501(c)(3) status?

No. State incorporation creates the nonprofit corporation. Federal tax-exempt recognition is a separate IRS process that generally requires an EIN and an appropriate exemption application.

What is Form 1023-EZ?

Form 1023-EZ is a streamlined IRS application available only to organizations that satisfy its eligibility requirements. Other organizations generally use Form 1023.

Does a nonprofit corporation need directors and bylaws?

Nonprofit corporations generally require directors under state law, and bylaws establish the organization’s internal governance procedures. Requirements vary by state and organization type.

Must a tax-exempt nonprofit file an annual return?

Most tax-exempt organizations have an annual Form 990-series filing obligation, although the required form and available exceptions depend on the organization’s status and circumstances.

Does USABA provide legal or tax advice?

USABA provides business consulting and coordination and can connect clients with independent legal and tax professionals when specialized advice is needed.
