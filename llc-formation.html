Frequently Asked Questions

What is an LLC?

A limited liability company is a flexible U.S. business entity that generally separates company liabilities from the owner's personal assets.

Which state should I form my LLC in?

The right state depends on where you operate, ownership, taxes, licensing, and practical requirements.

Does USABA provide legal or tax advice directly?

USABA coordinates business services and can connect clients with qualified independent professionals where needed.

How long does LLC formation take?

Processing time varies by state and filing method.

Can a non-U.S. person own an LLC?

Yes. Foreign ownership is generally permitted, subject to applicable tax, banking, licensing, and compliance requirements.
