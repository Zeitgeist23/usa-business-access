Frequently Asked Questions

What is an LLC?

An LLC is a limited liability company formed under state law. It can separate business obligations from its owners' personal assets when properly formed and maintained.

Which state should I form my LLC in?

The right state depends on where the business operates, where its owners live, and its legal, tax, and commercial goals.

How long does LLC formation take?

Timing varies by state and filing method. We explain expected processing times before the filing is submitted.

Can a non-U.S. person own an LLC?

Yes. A non-U.S. person can generally own a U.S. LLC, subject to applicable tax, banking, reporting, and compliance requirements.

Does USABA provide legal or tax advice?

USABA provides business consulting and coordination and can connect clients with independent legal and tax professionals when specialized advice is needed.
