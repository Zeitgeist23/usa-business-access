Frequently Asked Questions

What is an LLC?

A limited liability company is a flexible U.S. business entity that generally separates company liabilities from the owner's personal assets.

Which state should I form my LLC in?

The best state depends on where the business operates, ownership, taxes, licensing, and other practical considerations.

Does USABA provide legal or tax advice directly?

USABA coordinates business services and may connect clients with qualified independent attorneys, CPAs, tax professionals, and other providers where appropriate.

How long does LLC formation take?

Processing time varies by state and filing method. Expedited state filing may be available in some jurisdictions.
