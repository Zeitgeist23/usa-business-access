Frequently Asked Questions

What is an LLC?

An LLC is a limited liability company that separates business obligations from its owners' personal assets.

Which state should I form my LLC in?

The right state depends on where you operate, your ownership, and your business goals.

Does USAB provide legal or tax advice directly?

USABA coordinates with independent legal and tax professionals when specialized advice is needed.

How long does LLC formation take?

Timing varies by state and filing method. We explain the expected timeline before filing.

Can a non-U.S. person own an LLC?

Yes. A non-U.S. person can generally own a U.S. LLC, subject to applicable tax, banking, and compliance requirements.
